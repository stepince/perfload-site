curl-load Cloud Coming soon

Cloud-hosted orchestration for curl-load, without giving up control of your network. The dashboard, scheduling, and reporting run in curl-load's cloud — but the runner that actually sends the requests deploys inside your own VPC, giving it last-mile access to internal and private test targets that aren't reachable from the public internet. Same curl-first, no-script workflow you already use — nothing to expose publicly, no scripts to write.

What's coming

  • A hosted dashboard across projects and teams — no infrastructure to run yourself
  • A lightweight runner you deploy into your own VPC with a single command, for last-mile access to internal/private endpoints
  • Centralized results and comparative reports across every run, from every runner
  • Still built on k6 under the hood
