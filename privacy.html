Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

This policy covers the Curl Load Workspace Launcher Chrome extension.

Summary

This extension does not collect, store remotely, or transmit any user data. It has no analytics, no telemetry, and does not communicate with any server operated by us.

What the extension stores

The extension saves two settings — your configured Workbench URL and Runner Dashboard URL — using Chrome's local storage (chrome.storage.local). This data:

  • Stays on your device
  • Is never transmitted to us or any third party
  • Is only used to open the tabs you've configured when you click the extension

Network requests the extension makes

Before opening tabs, the extension sends a request to the Workbench and Dashboard URLs you configured, solely to check whether they're reachable. These requests go directly from your browser to the URLs you specified (typically your own local or self-hosted curl-load instance) — never to us.

Third parties

This extension does not use any third-party services, analytics providers, or advertising networks.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
